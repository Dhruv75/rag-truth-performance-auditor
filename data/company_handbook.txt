TECHCORP EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK 2026

SECTION 1: LEAVE POLICY

Employees receive 20 annual leave days per year.

Employees receive 12 sick leave days annually.

Unused leave may be carried forward up to 10 days.

Maternity leave entitlement is 26 weeks.

Paternity leave entitlement is 15 days.

Leave requests must be submitted at least 3 working days in advance.

SECTION 2: REMOTE WORK POLICY

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week.

Managers must approve fully remote arrangements.

Employees must maintain internet speeds above 50 Mbps while working remotely.

VPN access is mandatory for remote work.

Employees must attend all scheduled team meetings.

SECTION 3: TRAVEL POLICY

Travel reimbursement is limited to ₹5000 per trip.

Meal reimbursement is capped at ₹800 per day.

Hotel accommodation is capped at ₹4000 per night.

Business class travel is permitted only for directors and executives.

International travel requires department head approval.

Taxi reimbursement is capped at ₹1500 per day.

SECTION 4: IT POLICY

Laptop replacement cycle is every 4 years.

Employees must change passwords every 90 days.

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory.

Company devices may not be used for cryptocurrency mining.

Unauthorized software installation is prohibited.

SECTION 5: BENEFITS

Medical insurance coverage is ₹500000.

Internet allowance is ₹1000 per month.

Employees receive a learning budget of ₹25000 annually.

Gym membership reimbursement is capped at ₹12000 annually.

Quarterly performance bonuses are available.

SECTION 6: FINANCE POLICY

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days.

Procurement above ₹50000 requires director approval.

Petty cash reimbursement is limited to ₹2000.

Invoices must be retained for 7 years.

Vendor payments are processed within 15 business days.

SECTION 7: SECURITY POLICY

Employees must complete security awareness training every six months.

Company passwords must contain at least 12 characters.

Passwords must include uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters.

Sharing passwords with colleagues is strictly prohibited.

Employees must lock their devices when leaving their desks.

USB storage devices require manager approval before use.

Sensitive customer data must be encrypted at rest.

Sensitive customer data must be encrypted during transmission.

Security incidents must be reported within 2 hours of discovery.

Failed login attempts exceeding 5 attempts will result in temporary account suspension.

Employees may not access systems unrelated to their job responsibilities.

Annual penetration testing is conducted by external vendors.

SECTION 8: RECRUITMENT POLICY

All job openings must be approved by Human Resources.

Employee referrals receive a bonus of ₹10000 upon successful hiring.

Interview feedback must be submitted within 48 hours.

Background verification is mandatory for all full-time employees.

Probation period for new employees is 6 months.

Internship duration is typically 3 to 6 months.

Offer letters remain valid for 14 days.

Candidates must provide proof of identity before joining.

Technical interviews require a minimum of two interview rounds.

Hiring managers must complete interview training annually.

SECTION 9: TRAINING POLICY

Employees receive a learning budget of ₹25000 annually.

Certification exam fees may be reimbursed up to ₹15000 per year.

Mandatory compliance training must be completed within 30 days of assignment.

New employees must complete onboarding training within 14 days.

Managers must conduct quarterly skill development reviews.

Employees may attend up to 2 external conferences annually.

Conference attendance requires prior approval.

Training records must be retained for 5 years.

Leadership development programs are available for employees with over 2 years of tenure.

Employees who fail mandatory training may lose access to specific systems.

SECTION 10: PROCUREMENT POLICY

Procurement requests below ₹10000 may be approved by team leads.

Procurement requests above ₹50000 require director approval.

Vendor onboarding requires finance department verification.

A minimum of three vendor quotations is required for purchases above ₹100000.

Software purchases must be reviewed by the IT department.

Emergency purchases require post-facto approval within 3 business days.

Purchase orders remain valid for 90 days.

Contract renewals must be reviewed 60 days before expiration.

Procurement documentation must be retained for 7 years.

Conflict-of-interest declarations are mandatory during vendor selection.

SECTION 11: CODE OF CONDUCT

Employees must treat colleagues with respect and professionalism.

Harassment of any kind is strictly prohibited.

Discrimination based on gender, religion, ethnicity, or disability is prohibited.

Employees must disclose conflicts of interest.

Company assets must be used responsibly.

Confidential company information may not be shared externally without authorization.

Employees must comply with all applicable laws and regulations.

Gifts exceeding ₹5000 in value must be reported to management.

Employees may not accept bribes or improper payments.

Violations of the code of conduct may result in disciplinary action.

SECTION 12: DATA PRIVACY POLICY

Customer personal information must be processed lawfully.

Access to personal data must follow the principle of least privilege.

Customer data retention period is 5 years unless otherwise required by law.

Employees must report data breaches within 4 hours of discovery.

Personal information must not be stored on personal devices.

Data deletion requests must be processed within 30 days.

Access logs must be retained for 12 months.

Third-party vendors handling customer data must sign data processing agreements.

Data exports require manager approval.

Annual privacy compliance audits are mandatory.

SECTION 13: CUSTOMER SUPPORT POLICY

Support tickets must receive an initial response within 4 business hours.

Critical incidents require response within 1 hour.

Customer satisfaction surveys are sent after ticket closure.

Support interactions must be recorded in the CRM system.

Escalated tickets must be reviewed by a support manager.

Ticket resolution targets are 24 hours for high-priority issues.

Support records must be retained for 3 years.

SECTION 14: PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT POLICY

Performance reviews occur twice annually.

Employees are evaluated on goals, collaboration, and technical skills.

Managers must provide quarterly feedback sessions.

Promotion eligibility requires a minimum rating of 4 out of 5.

Performance improvement plans typically last 90 days.

Outstanding performers may receive spot bonuses.

Review documentation must be retained for 5 years.

SECTION 15: BUSINESS CONTINUITY POLICY

Critical systems must maintain 99.9% uptime.

Backups are performed daily.

Disaster recovery testing occurs every 6 months.

Recovery Time Objective (RTO) is 4 hours.

Recovery Point Objective (RPO) is 1 hour.

Emergency response teams must conduct annual drills.

Business continuity documentation is reviewed annually.
